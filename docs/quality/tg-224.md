# Pencil-beam machine QA intent

This note records the intention to cover pencil-beam scanning machine QA from logged session data, and the calculations that intention still lacks. It is not an IEC 62304 deliverable. It adds no software requirement id.

Scan Kit is pre-FDA and is not suitable for clinical use. [The safety classification](../software/safety-classification.md) is IEC 62304 Class A. Treatment-release judgment is outside the specified use. A person does not rely on Scan Kit as the record that a patient may be treated, or as the record that a machine may treat. Offering either of those uses reopens that classification before the behavior is added. The tolerances below are the recommendations of the cited report. They are not product specifications, and this software does not apply them as a release decision.

## Standards

Pencil-beam machine QA in this note follows AAPM TG-224, Arjomandy et al., “AAPM task group 224: Comprehensive proton therapy machine quality assurance,” Medical Physics 46, e678–e705 (2019), https://doi.org/10.1002/mp.13622. Only the pencil-beam scanning column of the daily, weekly, monthly, and annual tables is in scope.

Flatness, symmetry, and lateral penumbra use the definitions in ICRU Report 78 that TG-224 adopts. Lateral flatness is the spread of maximum and minimum dose over the central 80% of the field width. Lateral symmetry compares the two halves of that profile. Lateral penumbra is the 80% to 20% distance.

IAEA TRS-398 is cited only because TG-224 names it as the annual absolute output calibration. A session log cannot perform that calibration.

## Outside a logged delivery

These TG-224 activities are outside this note. They are not properties of a logged pencil-beam delivery:

- Double scattering and uniform scanning, including range-modulator wheels, apertures, and compensators.
- Multileaf collimators.
- Imaging, lasers, and x-ray to proton-beam coincidence, including the TG-142, TG-179, and MPPG 2.a checks TG-224 points to for cone-beam CT.
- Couch, gantry readout, snout position, and other mechanical tests.
- Room safety functional tests: door, audio, visual monitors, collision plates, search buttons, and radiation monitors.
- Activation surveys.
- Respiratory gating.
- Verification of daily QA equipment and cross-calibration of field chambers against an ADCL reference.

## What a session log already computes

These results are inputs. None of them is a TG-224 check. There is no stored baseline, no comparison against a tolerance, and no reported pass quantity.

- Dose versus requested MU, delivered dose per requested MU, dose rate, and dose ratios, in Bins. The menu is `crates/scan-kit-io/src/source.rs`.
- Position error versus the plan on each axis, that error with one nozzle offset removed, and the distance error of both. Sigma, sigma error in millimeters and as a percent of the expected width, and IC2 minus IC1. These are in Bins, and the axis errors are also in Distribution.
- Depth dose, lateral profile, and gamma in a phantom, in Volumetric (`crates/scan-kit-io/src/volumetric.rs`).
- Session-log errors, watchdog mismatches, and layer timing, in Session Log Compare (`crates/scan-kit-io/src/analysis/session_log.rs`).

## Checks a logged delivery can support

A later calculation has to add the missing piece named here. Until then the existing plot is only an input.

### Daily

TG-224 Table 1, pencil-beam scanning.

- **Output constancy, ±3% of baseline.** Dose error against requested MU, and delivered dose per requested MU, are plotted. The check needs a baseline dose per monitor unit at a named depth for one consistent field. Nozzle ionization-chamber dose can speak to monitor constancy. It is not a measurement with an external chamber.
- **Dose-rate constancy, ±2% of baseline.** Optional in the report. Dose rate is plotted. The check needs the same kind of baseline.
- **Distal range, ±1 mm from baseline at the distal 90% depth.** The report notes that some centers use 95% or 98%. Volumetric draws a depth dose. The check needs the distal depth read from that curve, the percent named in the definition, and a baseline depth. Optional proximal depth (±2 mm) and SOBP width (±2%, or ±2 mm) use the same curve.
- **Spot position, ±2 mm absolute and ±1 mm relative.** The log stores measured minus plan on each axis, that error with the mean nozzle offset removed, and the distance error of both. Imaging-isocenter absolute position is not in the log. A summary (maximum and root-mean-square, against the stated tolerance) is not computed.

### Weekly

TG-224 Table 2.

- **Review of daily results for drift.** No baseline series is stored, so a week of daily output or range cannot be reviewed inside the software.
- **Gantry angle (±1°), snout extension (±10 mm), and couch position (±1 mm and 1°).** These are mechanical readout checks. They are not in the dose tables.

### Monthly

TG-224 Table 3, pencil-beam scanning.

- **Output constancy, ±2% of baseline, at several gantry angles.** Same missing baseline as the daily output check. Delivered gantry angle is not a dose-table column, so results cannot be grouped by angle.
- **Field flatness ±2% and field symmetry ±1%, against baseline, at several gantry angles.** Neither quantity is computed. Both need a uniform scanned field and the ICRU 78 definitions.
- **Range, ±1 mm, for several energies that span the clinical range.** Same distal-depth extractor as the daily range check, repeated per energy.
- **Spot size, ±10% of baseline, at several gantry angles.** Sigma error is stored in millimeters and as a percent of the expected width for that delivery. It is not a stored QA baseline, and it is not grouped by gantry angle.
- **Interlocks on delivered dose, flatness, symmetry, spot position, and spot sigma.** Session Log Compare lists error issues from `SessionLogFile.log`. That list is not a structured interlock test.

### Annual

TG-224 Table 4, pencil-beam scanning.

- **Standard output calibration, ±2%, by IAEA TRS-398, with a dosimetry system calibrated by an Accredited Dosimetry Calibration Laboratory.** This is an independent measurement. The session log cannot perform it. Nozzle ionization-chamber dose remains a monitor-constancy input only.
- **Range, ±1 mm at the 90% depth, including off-axis beamlets and more than one gantry angle.** Same distal-depth gap as the daily check, plus off-axis depths.
- **Depth dose, ±2% maximum difference at any depth, against commissioning data.** The depth-dose curve is drawn. The maximum pointwise difference is not reported.
- **Lateral penumbra, ±2 mm on the 80% to 20% width, for selected beams at different depths and gantry angles.** Lateral profiles are drawn. The 80–20 width is not reported.
- **Range uniformity, ±0.5 mm, as the 90% depth at points off axis.** Off-axis distal depths are not extracted.
- **Field flatness ±2% and field symmetry ±1%.** Same gap as the monthly check.
- **Spot position, 1 mm absolute and 0.5 mm relative.** Same columns as the daily spot-position check, at the tighter tolerance. The absolute half needs the imaging isocenter. The relative half is the residual after one nozzle offset.
- **Spot size, ±10% of baseline, at several gantry angles.** Same gap as the monthly spot-size check.
- **Uniformity of spot shape, gamma at least 90% of pixels passing, at more than one gantry angle.** Volumetric gamma compares a dose volume with a plan or a TPS dose. It does not compare a spot-shape map with a QA baseline.
- **Monitor linearity ±1%, reproducibility ±2%, end effect, and minimum deliverable MU.** Dose versus MU is binned. The linearity residual, a comparison of repeated deliveries, and the end effect are not computed. Minimum and maximum spot MU can be read from the log once those limits are defined.
- **SOBP factors, range-shifter factors, and relative output factors, each ±2%.** Dose error versus energy is plotted, which is a step toward relative output. A range-shifter factor needs the shifter identity and the dose with and without it. Range-shifter water-equivalent thickness is only an input to the phantom calculation.
- **Inverse-square correction, ±1% from the effective source position.** Not computed.

## How a later calculation is specified

A change that adds one of the missing calculations follows [the software development plan](../software/software-development-plan.md): one `SK-REQ` id, one implementation, and a reference test recorded in the traceability table.

The written definition names the depth percent (90, unless the recorded center convention is 95 or 98), the frame, and the units. The result names the sessions, the baseline, and the software version. A tolerance is displayed with the result. Displaying it does not release the machine for treatment.
